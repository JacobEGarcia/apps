Watch Arcade Support

Watch Arcade is an offline Apple Watch game. Everything you need is on this page.

How to play

  • Reaction: Tap the instant the screen turns green; your best time is saved.
  • Pong: Steer your paddle with the Digital Crown. First to 5 wins.
  • Snake: Swipe to steer the classic apple chase.
  • 2048: Swipe to merge tiles on a 4x4 board.
  • Memory: Flip cards and match 8 emoji pairs in the fewest moves.
  • Tap Out: 20 seconds to hit as many blue targets as you can.

Your data

High scores live only on your watch. Deleting the app deletes them. There is no account and nothing to cancel.
