Noted Wild Support

Noted Wild is an iPhone journal for manually recording the details you notice outdoors. It is not a species-identification service, scientific database, safety guide, GPS tracker, or weather service.

Record an observation

Choose the compose control from Today or the plus control from Journal. A title and note are required to save. Date, time, place, weather, temperature, and subject tags are optional and entered manually. A new unfinished observation can remain as a local draft.

The journal supports up to 500 saved observations, with up to 12,000 characters in each observation body. At the limit, export your journal and remove older observations before adding another.

Search and organize

Journal searches titles, notes, places, manual condition text, and tags. Use the menus to narrow results by subject or place, or show favorites only. Field Index groups the subject tags and place names in your own journal.

Edit, favorite, remove, or undo

Open a saved observation to edit it or change its favorite status. Removing an observation offers an immediate Undo control. Once that undo opportunity is dismissed, restore the observation from an export if you have one.

Export

Settings can export the complete local snapshot as JSON or saved observations as Markdown. Exports are not redacted. JSON can include the current draft, journal text, manually entered places and conditions, tags, favorites, and timestamps. Review the file and destination before sharing.

Recovery mode

The current app source stores protected JSON files in the app’s Application Support directory and maintains a previous local snapshot. If the latest saved data is unreadable, Noted Wild attempts to open the last valid backup and preserves available recovery bytes. If durable restoration cannot be verified, editing is blocked to avoid overwriting recoverable data. Export the journal and recovery copy when available, or use Delete All for a clean start.

Delete all local data

In Settings, choose Delete all local data and confirm. This is designed to remove observations, the draft, backup, and preserved recovery copy from the app. It cannot remove files you previously exported elsewhere.

Troubleshooting information

When contacting support, include the Noted Wild version shown in Settings and your iPhone model and iOS version. Do not send an unredacted journal or recovery export unless you have reviewed it and intentionally choose to share it.
