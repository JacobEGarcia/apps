Privacy policy · Version 1.0

Renewal Exit keeps your ledger on your iPhone.

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Short version: Renewal Exit has no account, developer server, bank connection, analytics, advertising, or tracking. The developer does not collect your app data.

Information you enter

You may enter recurring-cost names, amounts, currencies, billing cycles, renewal dates, notes, exit actions, and cancellation status. This information is stored in the app’s local storage on your device.

No developer collection

Renewal Exit does not transmit your ledger to the developer. It does not create an account, connect to a bank or provider, use analytics or advertising SDKs, track you across apps or websites, or sell or share personal information.

Exports

The app prepares a JSON export only when you request one. iOS then lets you choose where to send or save it with the system share sheet. Your chosen destination may have its own privacy practices. The developer does not receive the export unless you deliberately send it to the support address.

Recovery archive

If the app finds local ledger bytes it cannot decode, it preserves those original bytes on the device so you can export a recovery archive. The app does not upload or automatically restore that archive.

Deleting data

Use More → Delete all local data to remove the ledger and recovery copy from the app. Deleting the app also removes its local container under normal iOS behavior. Copies you exported elsewhere must be deleted from those destinations separately.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. It is a general-audience personal utility.

Security and limitations

Local storage reduces data exposure, but no device or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect access to your iPhone and do not enter bank credentials, card numbers, passwords, or other secrets. Renewal Exit does not contact providers, verify prices, use exchange rates, cancel subscriptions, or provide financial advice.

Policy changes

If a future version changes its data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before those changes are released.
