Peak Hop Support

Peak Hop is an offline Apple Watch game. Everything you need is on this page.

How to play

  • Peak Hop: A 7-row isometric pyramid of 28 cubes. Hop a cube to change its color; turn them all orange to clear the level. Later levels need two hops per cube - and hopping a finished cube knocks it back. The Crown dials one of four diagonals; tap hops.

Contact

Email jacobegarcia101@gmail.com with your watch model and watchOS version, and we will get back to you.

Your data

High scores live only on your watch. Deleting the app deletes them. There is no account and nothing to cancel.
