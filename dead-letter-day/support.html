DEPOT SUPPORT DESK

Dead Letter Day Support

Support for version 1.0 (1), iPhone, iOS 17 or later.

How do I start?

Tap Clock In at the depot. Move with the directional control. Match the current letter’s address and shape to a mailbox, move close to it, and tap Deliver.

What ends a shift?

A route succeeds when the full delivery quota is complete. A timed shift is returned if the clock expires, route distortion reaches 100%, or five complaints are filed. A correct final delivery wins before those failure checks.

Scan, Dodge, and Break

Scan spends one charge to reveal the direct route and target highlight for six seconds. Dodge gives a short evasive burst in your current movement direction. Break at Bench lowers distortion when Milo is near the center bench and the cooldown is ready; timed routes spend four seconds for a break.

Quiet Route and Reduced Motion

Quiet Route removes the timer but keeps hazards, rewards, and progression. Its best score is tracked separately. Reduced Motion removes decorative shake, flashing, warping, pulsing, and dense effects without changing route rules. Both are available at the depot and in Settings.

Sound or haptics are unavailable

Check the Sound Effects and Haptics switches in Depot Settings, then confirm the iPhone’s system audio and haptic settings. The app uses short synthesized effects; system interruption behavior still requires physical-device verification.

Progress, export, and deletion

Progress is stored only on your device. In Depot Settings, Export Progress JSON opens the system share sheet with a complete local snapshot. Delete All Local Data permanently removes progress, settings, receipts, backups, recovery data, and prepared exports after confirmation. If the app detects unreadable storage, use Export Recovery Copy before erasing.
