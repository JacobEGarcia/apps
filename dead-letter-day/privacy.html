Dead Letter Day Privacy Policy

Effective: August 13, 2026

Dead Letter Day is designed as an offline, single-player game. The developer does not collect data from the app.

Information stored on your device

The app locally stores scores, merit stamps, upgrades, settings, recent shift receipts, and an optional paused route. This information is kept in the app’s protected storage on your device. It is not transmitted to the developer.

No accounts, tracking, or advertising

The app has no account system, analytics, advertising, tracking, social features, cloud sync, purchases, or developer-operated network service. It does not request location, contacts, health, photos, camera, or microphone access.

Exports

If you tap an export command, the app prepares a local JSON progress file or preserved recovery copy and opens Apple’s system share sheet. You choose whether and where to send it. The developer does not receive an export automatically. Review the destination’s privacy practices before sharing.

Deletion and recovery

Delete All Local Data in Depot Settings removes progress, upgrades, receipts, settings, paused-route data, backups, recovery data, and prepared export files. Uninstalling the app also removes its local app data according to iOS behavior. If stored data becomes unreadable, the app may preserve a local recovery copy and open in read-only mode so you can export it or erase and restart.

External services

Opening this policy, the support page, or an email link leaves the app. Apple, your browser, email provider, or hosting provider may process ordinary request information under their own policies. No game progress is attached automatically.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
