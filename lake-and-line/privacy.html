Lake & Line Privacy Policy

Draft effective date: August 13, 2026.

Summary

Lake & Line does not collect data. It has no account, backend, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDK.

On-device reading data

The app stores saved story identifiers, explicit read markers, and appearance preferences on your device. If normal storage cannot be recovered, it may preserve either a valid backup or unreadable raw bytes so you can export or erase them instead of silently overwriting data.

Exports and sharing

You may choose to export your complete local reading snapshot, a preserved recovery copy, or story-title text through Apple’s system interfaces. Exports are not redacted. A recovery copy may contain a valid backup or unreadable raw bytes. Review the selected content and destination before sharing. Lake & Line does not receive a copy.

External links

Opening this policy, support, or an email link leaves the app. The destination provider may process ordinary request metadata under its own policy. No reading data is attached automatically.

Deletion

Delete All removes the primary snapshot, backup, preserved recovery bytes, saved stories, read markers, and appearance preferences from the app’s local store.

Contact

Use the published Lake & Line support page. This draft must be hosted and verified before release.
