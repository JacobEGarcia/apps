Signal Hands: ASL Alphabet

Support

Signal Hands is a local American Manual Alphabet and introductory fingerspelling practice app for iPhone. It has no login or subscription.

Email support

Quick help

How do I resume a lesson?

Choose Save and close from the lesson. The Path and Settings screens show Resume exact saved challenge. Signal Hands restores the challenge, response, feedback, and shuffled order. Discard lesson permanently removes only that active lesson.

Why is a unit locked?

Units unlock in order. Complete the previous unit first. Completed units can be replayed.

How do retries and Review work?

An objective miss adds the letter to Review and can add at most one retry to that lesson. A self-check is reported separately and never counted as objective accuracy. Focused Review practices saved letters from one unit.

What do the dots under a letter mean?

They are local practice levels based on your own in-app activity. They are not a validated mastery, proficiency, or fluency score.

Why is the camera off?

Mirror Lab keeps the camera off until you tap Turn on camera and grant iOS permission. Signal Hands uses a private on-device preview only. It does not record, photograph, recognize, score, save, or transmit camera content, and it does not use the microphone.

How do I use a left-hand reference?

Open Settings and change Model hand to Left. The reference illustrations are mirrored. J and Z also include written movement cues because their motion cannot be learned from a still image alone.

What does the export contain?

Prepare complete progress export creates unredacted JSON containing the current local Signal Hands state and settings. Review the file and destination before sharing. Nothing is sent automatically.

How do I erase everything?

In Settings, choose Delete all Signal Hands data and confirm. This removes the primary progress file, local backup, preserved recovery bytes, and prepared exports. Copies already shared elsewhere are outside Signal Hands' control.

What if Signal Hands reports damaged progress?

Signal Hands first tries a validated local backup. If neither copy is usable, it preserves available recovery bytes and opens a read-only recovery screen where you can share those bytes for your records or erase the damaged state and start again.

Before contacting support

  • Include the Signal Hands version and iOS version.
  • Name the unit, letter, lesson prompt, or arcade mode.
  • Do not send an export unless you reviewed it and intentionally choose to share its complete contents.
  • Never send passwords, payment information, unrelated personal records, or camera images.

Instruction boundary

Signal Hands teaches the American Manual Alphabet and introductory fingerspelling, not all of ASL. It does not recognize or grade your signing and cannot certify fluency or proficiency. Learn complete ASL from qualified Deaf educators. For an instructional correction, email the exact letter, current cue, and recommended wording.
