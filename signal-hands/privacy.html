Signal Hands: ASL Alphabet

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Signal Hands does not collect your data.

The app has no account, developer backend, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDK. Practice activity is processed and stored on your iPhone.

Information stored on your device

Signal Hands stores XP, streak, completed units, per-letter practice levels, review letters, settings, the active lesson and feedback, and an active Sprint or Match round in its local app container. A protected primary file and validated local backup are excluded from ordinary device backup. If unreadable data is detected, available bytes are preserved locally and normal saves can be locked so they are not silently overwritten.

Optional camera mirror

The front camera is requested only after you tap Turn on camera in Mirror Lab. Signal Hands attaches a live video input to an on-device preview. It does not add a capture output, request microphone access, take photos, record video or audio, recognize hands, score signs, save frames, or transmit camera content. Leaving Mirror Lab or backgrounding the app stops the camera session.

Exports and sharing

Settings can prepare a complete JSON export of your local progress. Recovery mode can prepare preserved recovery bytes. These files are not redacted. Signal Hands shares nothing automatically: the iOS share sheet opens only after your action, and the destination you choose may process the file under its own terms. Review the destination and file before sharing.

Delete All

Delete All removes Signal Hands' primary progress file, local backup, preserved recovery bytes, and prepared temporary export files from the app container. It cannot recall a copy you previously shared elsewhere.

External links

Opening support, email, NIDCD, Gallaudet, or Wikimedia Commons leaves Signal Hands. The browser, mail provider, hosting provider, or selected destination may receive ordinary request or message metadata under its own privacy policy. Signal Hands does not attach practice progress to those links.

Children and education

Signal Hands is a general-audience practice tool and is not placed in the App Store Kids Category. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes and contact

If a future version changes this data boundary, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before release. Questions: jacobegarcia@icloud.com. Product help is available on the support page.
