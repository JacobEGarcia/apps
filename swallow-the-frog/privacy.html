Swallow the Frog

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

Swallow the Frog is designed to work privately on your iPhone. It does not require an account and does not send your tasks or activity to the developer.

Information the app stores

The app stores the information you enter or create, including task titles, first-bite descriptions, completion dates, released-frog records, and chronicle history. This information is kept in the app’s private local storage on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information, task content, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, diagnostics, or tracking data through the app. The app has no developer-operated account system, cloud backend, analytics service, advertising service, or tracking SDK.

Network use

The app’s core functionality does not require a network connection. If you choose a privacy, support, or email link, iOS opens the relevant browser or email app. Those external services operate under their own privacy policies.

JSON export

You can ask the app to prepare a readable JSON export of your tasks and chronicle. The export is created locally with iOS file protection and presented through the standard iOS share sheet. Prepared temporary files are removed when replaced, when you leave More, and during Delete All. You decide whether and where to share an export. Once you send or save it outside the app, its handling is controlled by the destination you selected.

Retention and deletion

Your local information remains on your device until you edit it, delete it in the app, use Delete all local data, or remove the app. Delete all removes the app’s stored task and chronicle archive. Deleting the app also removes its app-container data under normal iOS behavior. Copies you previously exported are not deleted by the app.

Tracking and advertising

Swallow the Frog does not track you, build an advertising profile, display advertising, or sell or share personal information.

Children

The app is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed specifically to children. Because the app does not collect information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Your information receives the protections provided by the iOS app sandbox and your device security. No method of storage is guaranteed to be completely secure, so protect access to your device and handle exported files carefully.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before the new practices are introduced. The effective date above identifies the current version.
