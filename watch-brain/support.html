Watch Brain Support

Watch Brain is an offline Apple Watch game. Everything you need is on this page.

How to play

  • Sequence: Working memory: repeat a growing light pattern.
  • Math Dash: Processing speed: arithmetic against a shrinking timer bar.
  • Pattern Recall: Spatial memory: memorize lit cells on a 3x3 grid, tap them back.
  • Stroop: Attention control: the word lies - tap the INK color.

Contact

Your data

High scores live only on your watch. Deleting the app deletes them. There is no account and nothing to cancel.
