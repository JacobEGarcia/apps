Privacy policy · Version 1.0

Your run record stays on your iPhone.

Effective date: August 12, 2026

Short version: Run Clubbed has no account, developer server, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or tracking. The developer does not collect your app data.

Information you enter

You may enter runner names and notes, club-run titles, dates and times, meeting points, target distances, unverified local RSVP marks, recaps, run notes, effort, and optional manual distance. The app also stores timer state and saved run receipts. This information remains in local app storage on your device.

No location, health, contacts, or social access

Version 1.0 does not use GPS, foreground or background location, HealthKit, contacts, motion sensors, invitations, messaging, social accounts, or live attendance. Meeting points and distances are text or numbers you enter manually.

No developer collection

Run Clubbed does not transmit your roster, plans, timer, or receipts to the developer. It does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, track you across apps or websites, or sell or share personal information.

Exports

A JSON export is prepared only when you request one. iOS then lets you choose where to send or save it with the system share sheet. Your chosen destination may have separate privacy practices. The developer does not receive your export unless you deliberately send it to the support address.

Recovery archive

If the app finds stored archive bytes it cannot decode, it preserves those original bytes locally and never includes them in an export. Version 1.0 does not upload or automatically repair that recovery snapshot.

Deleting data

Use More → Delete all local data to remove the app’s roster, events, receipts, active timer, and recovery copy. Deleting the app also removes its local container under normal iOS behavior. Copies you exported elsewhere must be deleted separately.

Names and permission

Add other people’s names only with their permission. A roster entry is a private note on your device; it does not create an account, contact the person, or verify their identity or attendance.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. It is a general-audience personal utility.

Security and safety limits

Local storage reduces exposure, but no device or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Run Clubbed does not monitor emergencies, verify locations, or provide medical, coaching, or safety advice. Do not rely on it to contact anyone. Share plans directly and use appropriate safety judgment.

Policy changes

If a future version changes its data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before those changes are released.
