The City Listens: Latin · Version 1.0

Privacy policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Short version: The City Listens is designed to work without an account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or a developer backend. Your learning progress stays in app-scoped storage on your iPhone unless you intentionally export it.

Data stored on your device

The app stores a versioned local progress archive. It may include completed encounter identifiers, answer-attempt counts, review items, mosaic points, practice streak and day, display preferences, onboarding state, and an in-progress encounter with its stable answer order and current feedback.

The archive is written to Application Support storage with a last-known-good backup. The app requests iOS data protection for its files and backup exclusion for its storage directory; those platform attributes remain subject to final physical-device and archive verification. If saved data cannot be safely decoded or restored, the app preserves recovery bytes when possible and can enter a read-only state so uncertain data is not silently overwritten.

Export and deletion

You can prepare a complete JSON export in More. It contains the selected local progress archive and is not redacted. The file leaves the app only after you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet.

Delete All removes the progress file, backup, recovery state, local preferences, and prepared temporary export files. Deleting the app also removes its app-scoped local data.

Data collection and tracking

The inspected version contains no developer account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking, cloud synchronization, artificial-intelligence service, in-app purchase, or remote course download. It does not request location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health, or speech-recognition access.

The proposed App Store privacy response is Data Not Collected. That response must be reconciled with the final signed archive and included SDKs before release.

Audio and accessibility preferences

Optional correct and incorrect feedback tones are synthesized on the device. The app does not record audio. Reduced-motion and macron-display preferences are stored only as part of the local archive.

External links

The fixed Privacy Policy and Support links open only after you tap them. Your browser and website host may process ordinary request information under their own terms. The app does not automatically attach your progress, answers, exports, or recovery data to those requests.

Educational boundary

The City Listens provides a bundled beginner Classical Latin practice experience. It is not certified instruction, a complete course, or a measurement of mastery or fluency.
