Let the endings speak.

Help with the Journey, sentence building, review, Codex, local progress, recovery, comfort controls, and data deletion.

What is The City Listens?

It is an offline practice game with five fictional Roman districts, fifteen bundled beginner Classical Latin morphology encounters, and a 41-entry Codex. It is not certified instruction or a complete Latin course.

How do I begin?

Finish the short introduction, open Journey, then choose an encounter in The Gate. Districts unlock in order after all three encounters in the previous district are completed.

Why can’t I check a sentence yet?

A Build encounter requires every tile before Check becomes available. Tap a selected tile to remove it, then arrange the complete sentence. The grammar rule and hint remain beside the challenge.

How does Review work?

An encounter answered incorrectly is added once to Review. It remains there until you answer it correctly. Review is explicit practice, not artificial intelligence, adaptive scheduling, or spaced repetition.

What does “encountered” mean in the Codex?

It means the word appeared in a completed encounter. It does not mean the word was mastered, recalled later, or independently assessed. All 41 reference entries are readable from the start.

Will my active encounter survive relaunch?

The app saves the encounter, stable shuffled answer order, current selection, and submitted feedback state locally. If a save fails, the visible mutation is rolled back instead of being reported as durable progress.

Why are changes disabled?

If local files cannot be safely read or restored, the app can enter read-only recovery so uncertain bytes are not overwritten. Open More to prepare any available recovery copy or complete progress export. Delete All is the deliberate way to discard the local files and begin again.

What is included in an export?

The prepared JSON contains the complete local progress archive selected in the app, including progress, attempts, settings, and any in-progress encounter. It is not redacted. Nothing leaves until you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet.

What does Delete All remove?

It removes local progress, preferences, backup and recovery files, read-only state, and prepared temporary exports. The operation is confirmed before it runs and cannot be undone.

What do the comfort controls change?

Feedback tones enable short synthesized correct and incorrect sounds. Reduce Decorative Motion removes nonessential movement. Show Macrons changes displayed text only; it does not change answer identity or grading.

Does the app need the internet or an account?

No feature requires an account or internet connection. The fixed privacy and support links open in your browser only after you tap them.
