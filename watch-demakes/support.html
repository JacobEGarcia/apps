Watch Demakes Support

Watch Demakes is an offline Apple Watch game. Everything you need is on this page.

How to play

  • Blockfall: Falling-block stacking. Crown slides, tap rotates, swipe down hard-drops. Ghost piece shown.
  • Tap Wings: One-button flier. Tap to flap through the pipes.
  • Brickline: Brick-breaking paddle action. Crown moves the paddle, 3 lives, levels ramp speed.
  • Sky Bounce: Vertical platform climber. Crown drifts, auto-bounce, the camera follows you up.

Contact

Email jacobegarcia101@gmail.com with your watch model and watchOS version, and we will get back to you.

Your data

High scores live only on your watch. Deleting the app deletes them. There is no account and nothing to cancel.
