IRONWOOD YARD SHIFT

Privacy Policy

Effective August 13, 2026

Short version: Ironwood Yard Shift has no account, ads, analytics, tracking, purchases, or gameplay network client. Your record and preferences stay on your device.

Information the app stores

Ironwood Yard Shift stores your best score, best grade, and preferences for sound, haptics, Reduced Motion, and assist controls. This information is stored only inside the app's private container on your device.

If a saved record cannot be read, the app may preserve those unreadable local bytes in its private container while opening clean defaults. You can remove both the active record and any recovery copy with the in-app delete command.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal data or gameplay data. The app does not transmit your score, grade, settings, activity, device identifiers, diagnostics, or usage information to the developer or to an analytics service.

Accounts, advertising, and tracking

The app does not provide accounts or sign-in. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking technology, social features, cloud sync, or in-app purchases.

Device permissions

The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, health data, motion data, Bluetooth, or notifications. Short sound effects and haptics are generated on the device and can be disabled independently.

Network access and external links

Gameplay works offline and the app has no gameplay network client. Settings includes links to this privacy policy and the support page. Those destinations open only when you choose them. Your browser and the destination host may apply their own privacy practices outside the app.

Data retention and deletion

Local records and preferences remain on your device until you delete them, remove the app, or erase the device. To delete them inside Ironwood Yard Shift, open Depot Settings, choose Delete local records and preferences, and confirm. The deletion cannot be undone.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else. Ironwood Yard Shift is a general-audience arcade game and is not submitted to the App Store Kids Category.

Safety and product scope

Ironwood Yard Shift is an arcade logistics game. It is not forklift training, workplace safety instruction, or certification, and its scores and grades are not workplace records.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released. The effective date at the top identifies the current policy.

Contact

For privacy questions or app support, visit the Ironwood Yard Shift support page.
