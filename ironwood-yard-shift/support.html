IRONWOOD YARD SHIFT

Support

Help for the offline three-minute arcade logistics game.

Version 1.0 (1) · Requires an iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

How a shift works

  1. Tap Clock In to start the countdown and three-minute shift.
  2. Drive toward the highlighted load. Center both forks and raise them to secure the pallet.
  3. Carry the load low and steady to the bay with the same symbol.
  4. Lower the pallet into the bay, then reverse clear to complete the delivery.
  5. Protect your safety score and chain multiplier until the horn.

Controls

Driving

Press and hold Forward, Reverse, Steer Left, Steer Right, or Brake. Tap-to-toggle assist controls can make each command stay active until you tap it again.

Forks and camera

Raise or lower the forks with the right-side controls. Cycle among Overhead, Chase, and Precision views from the current-job panel.

Recover

Recover returns the forklift and current pallet to the safe lane. It deducts 250 points, reduces safety by eight points, and resets the chain.

Comfort and accessibility

Open Depot Settings to change sound, haptics, Reduced Motion, and tap-to-toggle assist controls. Reduced Motion keeps the camera in a stable overhead view and removes the pulsing objective marker.

Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Gameplay and local records work offline. Internet access is needed only if you choose to open the privacy or support webpage.

Why did my load drop?

Speed, sharp steering, and high forks increase instability. Slow down, straighten the forklift, and carry the pallet close to the ground.

Why did delivery not complete?

Lower the pallet fully inside its matching symbol bay, release it, then reverse until the forklift is clear.

Where are my records?

Your best score, best grade, and preferences stay inside the app on this device. There is no account, cloud sync, or cross-device restore.

How do I delete local data?

Open Depot Settings, choose Delete local records and preferences, and confirm. This removes the active record, preferences, and any local recovery copy.

Troubleshooting

  • If a drive or fork command remains active, pause the shift. Pausing releases held controls.
  • If sound is unavailable, verify Sound Effects in Depot Settings and check the iPhone's current audio route.
  • If haptics are unavailable, verify Haptics in Depot Settings and the iPhone's system haptic settings.
  • If the interface becomes unresponsive, close and reopen the app. Unfinished shift progress is not saved.

Privacy

Ironwood Yard Shift has no account, ads, analytics, tracking, purchases, or gameplay network client. Read the full privacy policy.

Contact the developer

For a problem not covered here, contact the developer through the public JacobEGarcia GitHub profile. Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, what you expected, and the steps that reproduced the issue. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal information.

Important scope

Ironwood Yard Shift is an arcade game. It is not forklift training, workplace safety instruction, or certification.
