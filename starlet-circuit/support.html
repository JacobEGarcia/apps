How do the race controls work?

Tap left, right, or boost for a short pulse, or press and hold for continuous input. Steering at speed charges drift. Tap the prism item when it lights up. Use Pause to resume, restart, or exit.

What happens if I leave the app during a race?

The race pauses when Starlet Circuit is no longer active. Steering and boost input are cleared. Return to the app and choose Resume, Restart Race, or Exit to Lobby.

How do racer unlock odds work?

The displayed rarity odds are S+ 8%, S 26%, and A 66%. An unlock uses one local ticket or 1,000 local coins. A duplicate returns 800 coins for S+, 450 for S, or 250 for A.

Can I buy coins, tickets, racers, or prizes?

No. There are no purchases, in-app purchases, ads, prizes, trading, cash value, or cash-out. The app begins with a local starter balance and completed races award local coins and XP.

Why are changes blocked?

The primary progress snapshot could not be decoded and no valid backup was available. Starlet Circuit preserves the uncertain bytes and shows starter progress read-only. Use Erase All Data only if you intend to discard all primary, backup, recovery, and legacy local data.

What does Export Progress JSON include?

It is a full copy of current coins, tickets, XP, unlocked racers, selected racer, tune levels, and schema version. You choose its destination through iOS. Review the file before sharing it.

What is the difference between Reset and Erase?

When storage is writable, Reset Progress starts fresh active progress, but the prior primary can remain as a local rolling backup. Erase All Data removes primary, backup, recovery, and legacy keys before restoring fresh progress.

Does Starlet Circuit have sound?

No. Version 1.0 has no audio. Haptics provide optional local feedback, and the race remains playable without them.
