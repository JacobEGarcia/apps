Afterword Commonplace 1.0 · iPhone · iOS 17 or later

Support

Afterword Commonplace is an offline poetry-reading ritual and private commonplace book.

Quick start

  1. Open Tonight and draw one of twelve sourced voices.
  2. Read the excerpt, lens, and prompt. Save the excerpt or write a response.
  3. Find saved pages in Book, or browse every voice in Archive.
  4. Use Circle to arrange one to three voices and prepare a plain-text agenda.

Common questions

Does Circle invite people or reserve a room?

No. Circle prepares local text. You decide whether and where to share it. The app does not send invitations, reserve a place, operate a membership group, or confirm delivery.

Where is my writing stored?

In the app’s local container on your iPhone. There is no account, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, tracking, or developer server.

How do I back up my commonplace?

Open More, prepare JSON or readable Markdown, then choose a destination in Apple’s share sheet. JSON is the complete machine-readable snapshot. Markdown is intended for reading. Exports contain the writing you selected to save and are not redacted.

What is read-only recovery?

If durable storage cannot safely accept changes, the app may keep a verified snapshot open without allowing edits. Open More to export the readable state or preserved bytes, then use Delete All only after you have secured anything you need.

How do I delete everything?

Open More → Delete all local data and confirm. This removes entries, the circle plan, preferences, backups, recovery bytes, markers, and prepared exports. It cannot be undone.

Why does a source page fail to load?

Source links belong to external publishers and may change, block automated traffic, or require a network connection. The included excerpt, attribution, publication basis, and rights note remain available in the app.

Accessibility and comfort

The app supports Dynamic Type, system appearance, VoiceOver-oriented labels and reading order, and a Reduce Motion preference. Physical-device testing remains part of each release’s final quality process.
