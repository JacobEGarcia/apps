Afterword Commonplace Privacy Policy

Afterword Commonplace is designed so your reading and writing stay on your iPhone.

Data collection

Afterword Commonplace does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or use your data for tracking. The app has no account, backend, analytics, advertising, tracking technology, artificial-intelligence service, or third-party software development kit.

Information stored on your device

The app can store saved excerpts, your responses and personal lines, favorites, a reading-circle plan, appearance settings, and recovery information in its local app container. This information is used only to provide the features you request.

If a saved file is unreadable, the app may preserve the original bytes locally and open a verified backup or a read-only recovery state. This is intended to reduce accidental data loss.

Sharing and exports

You can prepare a complete JSON backup, readable Markdown, a plain-text circle agenda, or preserved recovery bytes. These files leave the app only when you invoke Apple’s share sheet and select a destination. Exports are not redacted; review them before sharing. Prepared temporary exports are removed when their screen closes, at the next app launch, and by Delete All.

External links

Source, privacy, and support links open only after you tap them. Opening a web page or email leaves the app, and the destination or platform provider may process ordinary request information under its own policy. Afterword Commonplace does not automatically attach your entries or circle plan.

Deletion

Use More → Delete all local data to remove the commonplace, circle plan, preferences, primary and backup files, preserved recovery bytes, recovery markers, and prepared exports. Uninstalling the app also removes its local app container, subject to Apple platform behavior and any device backup you control.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact
