Watch Arcade 3D Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Summary

Watch Arcade 3D collects no personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no purchases. The game runs entirely on your Apple Watch.

Data stored on your device

High scores and settings are stored locally on your watch. They never leave the device on their own and are deleted when you delete the app.

Data shared by you

If you email support, we receive your email address and message and use them only to respond.

Third parties

Watch Arcade 3D integrates no third-party SDKs and shares no data with third parties.
