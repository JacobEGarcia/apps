Watch Arcade 3D Support

Watch Arcade 3D is an offline Apple Watch game. Everything you need is on this page.

How to play

  • Crown Run: A 3D endless dodger on a neon track. Speed ramps forever; the Digital Crown steers.
  • Stack Tower: Drop blocks to build a tower. Overhang gets sliced off and the camera orbits as you climb.
  • Space Strike: A 3D asteroid shooter. Crown aims, tap fires.

Contact

Email jacobegarcia101@gmail.com with your watch model and watchOS version, and we will get back to you.

Your data

High scores live only on your watch. Deleting the app deletes them. There is no account and nothing to cancel.
