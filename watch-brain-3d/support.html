Watch Brain 3D Support

Watch Brain 3D is an offline Apple Watch game. Everything you need is on this page.

How to play

  • Block Count: Spatial working memory: a 3D cube pile spins for 5 seconds - dial in how many cubes it held.
  • Maze Roll: Planning: a real 3D tilt maze on a generated layout. Roll the ball into the goal ring.
  • Odd Cube: Visual attention: one cube in a spinning cluster is a slightly different shade, shrinking each round.

Contact

Your data

High scores live only on your watch. Deleting the app deletes them. There is no account and nothing to cancel.
