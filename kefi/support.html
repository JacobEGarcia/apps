Support

Kefi is a local beginner Modern Greek practice app for iPhone. It has no login or subscription.

Quick help

How do I resume a lesson?

Choose Save and Close from the lesson. Then open Profile and tap Resume saved lesson. Kefi restores the exact exercise, response, feedback, and shuffled option order. Discard saved lesson permanently removes only that active lesson.

Why is a lesson locked?

Lessons unlock in order. Complete the previous lesson first. Completed lessons can be replayed.

How do hearts and review work?

A wrong answer costs one of five daily hearts, adds the exercise to Review, and places one retry at the end of the lesson. Hearts reset with the local day. If they reach zero, the free refill restores five so the lesson can continue. Review starts a focused session for saved mistakes from one lesson.

Why can I not hear Greek speech?

Check Profile → Greek speech, device volume, and whether an iPhone Greek system voice is available. Playback begins only when you tap it. Kefi includes no audio recordings and cannot guarantee which system voice is installed. Leaving the lesson or backgrounding the app stops speech.

What does the export contain?

Prepare progress export creates complete, unredacted JSON containing your current Kefi learning state and preferences. Review the file and destination before sharing. Nothing is sent automatically.

How do I erase everything?

In Profile, choose Delete all Kefi data and confirm. This removes the primary progress file, local backup, preserved recovery bytes, and prepared exports. Copies you already shared elsewhere are outside Kefi's control.

What if Kefi reports damaged progress?

Kefi first tries a valid local backup. If neither copy is usable, it preserves available recovery bytes and opens a recovery screen where you can share those bytes for your own records or erase the damaged local state and start again.

Before contacting support

  • Include the Kefi version and iOS version.
  • Describe the lesson and exercise type.
  • Do not send a progress or recovery export unless you have reviewed it and intentionally choose to share its full contents.
  • Never send passwords, payment information, or unrelated personal records.

Course boundary

Kefi is a bounded beginner-practice tool, not certified instruction, translation advice, or a guarantee of fluency. For an incorrect or culturally awkward lesson item, email the exact on-screen phrase and expected correction.
