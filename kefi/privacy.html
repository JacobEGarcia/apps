Kefi Greek Practice

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Kefi does not collect your data.

The app has no account, developer backend, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDK. Learning activity is processed and stored on your iPhone.

Information stored on your device

Kefi stores lesson progress, XP, daily goal, hearts, streak, completed lessons, review items, the active lesson and response, and practice preferences in its app container. The primary progress file and local backup use iOS file protection and are excluded from ordinary device backup. If Kefi detects unreadable data, it can preserve recovery bytes locally and enter a read-only recovery state rather than silently overwrite them.

Speech

When you tap a listening control, Kefi asks the iPhone's system speech synthesizer to speak Greek text already bundled in the lesson. Kefi does not record audio, access the microphone, perform speech recognition, or send lesson responses to the developer. System voice availability and operating-system behavior are controlled by Apple and the device.

Exports and sharing

Profile can prepare a complete JSON export of your local learning state. Recovery mode can prepare the preserved recovery bytes. These exports are not redacted. Kefi shares nothing automatically: the iOS share sheet opens only after your action, and the destination you choose may process the file under its own terms. Review the destination and file before sharing.

Delete All

Delete All removes Kefi's primary progress file, local backup, preserved recovery bytes, and prepared temporary export files from the app container. It cannot recall a copy you previously shared to another app, person, or service.

External links

Opening the support page or email link leaves Kefi. Your browser, mail provider, hosting provider, or selected destination may receive ordinary request or message metadata under its own privacy policy. Kefi does not attach learning progress to those links.

Children and education

Kefi is a general-audience beginner practice tool and is not placed in the App Store Kids Category. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes and contact

If a future version changes the data boundary, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before release. Questions: jacobegarcia@icloud.com. Product help is available on the support page.
