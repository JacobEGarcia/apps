Ownfold privacy

Your workspace belongs to you. Ownfold 1.0 does not create an account, send your notes to a developer server, or embed analytics, advertising, tracking, or AI services.

Data stored on your device

Ownfold stores the notes, tasks, saved link addresses, Projects, Areas, tags, due dates, preferences, backups, and recovery copies that you create. These files are kept in the app’s protected local Application Support container. They remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Data we collect

We do not collect data from Ownfold. There is no Ownfold account, cloud service, developer database, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or tracking technology in version 1.0.

Exports and external links

You can prepare a complete JSON backup or readable Markdown export and share it through Apple’s system share sheet. The export contains the workspace content you selected; it is not redacted. Review it before choosing a recipient. Ownfold does not send it automatically.

When you open a saved web link, this privacy page, or the support page, you leave Ownfold. Your browser, the destination site, network provider, and platform provider may process ordinary request information under their own policies. Ownfold does not attach a note or task to those requests.

Recovery and deletion

If a saved workspace cannot be validated, Ownfold preserves available bytes and opens a safe read-only recovery state instead of silently overwriting them. Settings lets you export that recovery copy.

Delete all local data removes the workspace, backup, recovery files, preferences, and prepared temporary exports. Removing the app also removes its local container according to iOS behavior. A file you previously shared is controlled by its destination and is not deleted by Ownfold.

Children and sensitive information

Ownfold is a general productivity utility, not a service directed to children. Because entries are free-form, avoid storing passwords, payment credentials, government identifiers, medical records, or other information that requires a dedicated secure system.

Changes and contact

If a future version changes this data boundary, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before that version is released.
