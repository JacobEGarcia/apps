Practical help for task entry, clipboard import, random selection, focus sprints, sound, recovery, and local data controls.

How does TASKPOT choose a task?

It randomly selects from tasks that are currently enabled. If more than one task is enabled, it avoids immediately repeating the currently selected task. TASKPOT does not claim a certified drawing, fairness guarantee, or equal long-run outcome.

Is this gambling?

No. The reels, pull control, jackpot language, wins, and celebration are a productivity theme. TASKPOT has no money, wagering, odds, prizes, redeemable rewards, purchases, or real gambling.

How do I paste tasks from Notes?

Copy checklist lines or plain text in Notes. Return to TASKPOT, open Tasks, open the import menu, then tap Paste from Notes. TASKPOT reads clipboard text only after that tap. You can also choose Type or paste a list and enter one task per line.

What task-list formats work?

Plain lines, bullets, numbered lines, checkboxes, and common checkmarks are accepted. Empty and duplicate lines are skipped. A single bulk import accepts up to 100 unique lines, and task titles are limited to 180 characters.

Can I choose the sprint length?

Yes. Open Wins, then Settings, and choose 5, 10, 15, or 25 minutes. Starting a sprint is optional; you can mark the selected task done without starting one.

Why did music, sound, or haptics stop?

Check the three independent controls under Settings. TASKPOT stops its synthesized audio when it leaves the foreground. Device silent-mode, volume, audio-session, or haptic settings may also affect feedback.

Why are changes disabled?

If TASKPOT cannot decode saved data, it preserves a recovery copy and becomes read-only so it does not overwrite uncertain bytes. The starter state shown in that condition is not a successful recovery of the unreadable tasks. Use Erase all data only if you intend to discard the saved and recovery data and start over.

What is included in JSON export?

The export contains the full current decodable snapshot: tasks, wins, preferences, selected-task state, and active sprint timestamps. It does not include preserved corrupt recovery bytes. Review the file before choosing an external destination because it is not redacted.

What do clear and erase remove?

Clear win history removes completion records only. Erase all data removes tasks, wins, settings, sprint state, and recovery data, then restores starter tasks. Files previously exported elsewhere are not removed.

Does TASKPOT use AI, an account, or cloud sync?

No. Version 1.0 uses local app logic and app-scoped preferences. It has no AI generation, account, or app-operated cloud-sync service. iOS backups or device transfer may handle app data according to your system settings.

Where are the privacy policy and support links in the app?

Open Wins, tap the Settings button, then scroll to Privacy. The fixed Privacy policy and Support links open these public pages in your browser. TASKPOT does not attach task data to either request.
