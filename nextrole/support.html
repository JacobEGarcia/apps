Next Role Support

Next Role is an offline app. Your skills and target roles live on your device.

How it works

  • Add your skills and honest levels (0-5) under Skills; log practice hours with +1h.
  • Add target roles under Roles; each carries a required bar per skill.
  • The Match tab computes your role match, draws the skill map, and ranks the gaps to close.

Contact

Your data

Your career data lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. There is no account and nothing to cancel.
