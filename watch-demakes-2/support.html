Watch Demakes 2 Support

Watch Demakes 2 is an offline Apple Watch game. Everything you need is on this page.

How to play

  • Salvage Run: Vector rock breaker with drift physics and wrap-around space. Crown steers, hold to thrust, tap fires.
  • Midnight Maze: Dot-chase maze with two hunters. Power dots turn the tables. Crown dials your next turn.
  • Formation Zero: Dive-bomb waves. The formation sways and divers swoop for double points. Crown slides, tap fires.
  • Rush Hour: Lane crossing. Traffic speeds up every crossing. Tap hops forward, Crown sidesteps.

Contact

Email jacobegarcia101@gmail.com with your watch model and watchOS version, and we will get back to you.

Your data

High scores live only on your watch. Deleting the app deletes them. There is no account and nothing to cancel.
