Ship or Die · Privacy

Nothing leaves the ship.

Effective date: August 12, 2026

Ship or Die is designed as a local-first iPhone app. It does not require an account and does not operate a server that receives your tasks, focus history, or vitality checkmarks.

Data the app stores

The app stores tasks, completed focus sessions, onboarding state, vitality checkmarks, and widget state on your device. The included widgets access only the app’s shared App Group container.

Data collection and sharing

Ship or Die does not collect, sell, rent, transmit, or share personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics SDK, third-party tracking SDK, or cloud database.

Notifications

If you grant permission, iOS schedules and delivers optional reminders on your device. You can disable notification access in iPhone Settings at any time.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you erase it inside the app or uninstall the app. To delete it in the app, open Vitality → Privacy & Support → Erase all local data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children or anyone else because it does not collect user data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date and this page will be updated before the changed data practice is introduced.
