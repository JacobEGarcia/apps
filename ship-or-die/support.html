Ship or Die · Support

Get the ship moving again.

Ship or Die is an offline, account-free iPhone focus and planning app. Support requests are handled directly by the developer.

Email support

Notifications are missing

Open the app, go to Vitality → Privacy & Support, and choose Open notification settings. Confirm that notifications are allowed.

Widgets are not updating

Open Ship or Die once after installing or updating it, make a small change such as completing a vitality item, then return to the Home Screen. If needed, remove and add the widget again.

Erase all app data

Open Vitality → Privacy & Support → Erase all local data. This removes tasks, focus history, checkmarks, shared widget state, and scheduled reminders from the device.

What to include in a bug report

Please include the iPhone model, iOS version, the screen you were using, what you expected, and what happened. Do not send private task content unless it is necessary to reproduce the issue.
