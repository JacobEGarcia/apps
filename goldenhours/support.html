Golden Hours Support

Golden Hours is an offline app. Your weekly plan lives on your device.

How it works

  • Plan golden hours across five themes: Create, Move, Connect, Help, Rest.
  • Tap a block to mark it done; tap a day column to see its hours.
  • Add your own hours from the Add tab, or let Find a free slot place one.

Contact

Email jacobegarcia101@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version, and we will get back to you.

Your data

Your plan lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. There is no account and nothing to cancel.
