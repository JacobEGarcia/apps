Capacity Support

Capacity is an offline app. Your quests, XP and hearts live on your device.

How it works

  • Three daily quests refresh at midnight; the weekly boss quest resets each week.
  • Tap a quest to mark it done and earn XP and hearts; tap again to undo.
  • Add your own quests under My Quests.

Contact

Email jacobegarcia101@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version, and we will get back to you.

Your data

Progress lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. There is no account and nothing to cancel.
