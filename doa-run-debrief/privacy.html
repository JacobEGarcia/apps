Privacy policy · Version 1.0

Your debrief stays on your iPhone.

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Short version: DOA: Run Debrief is designed without an account, developer server, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or tracking. The developer does not collect your app data.

Information you enter

You may record a run outcome, intended and actual distance or time, effort, friction tags, what happened, what worked, and one adjustment. You may also choose miles or kilometers. This information and any pattern counts remain in local app storage on your device.

No run or health tracking

Version 1.0 does not use a run timer, GPS, foreground or background location, routes, HealthKit, Apple Watch, motion sensors, contacts, or medical data sources. Every run detail is an optional manual entry.

No developer collection

The app does not transmit debriefs or preferences to the developer. It does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, track you across apps or websites, sell personal information, or share app data with a developer backend.

Exports

A JSON export is prepared only when you request one. It is a complete, unredacted copy of the selected stored fields, including reflection text—not a sanitized summary. Review it before sharing. iOS then lets you choose where to send or save it with the system share sheet. Your chosen destination may have separate privacy practices. The developer does not receive an export unless you deliberately send it to the support address.

Local backup and recovery

The app keeps the prior valid archive as a local backup. If the primary archive becomes unreadable, the app preserves those unreadable bytes in a separate local recovery snapshot and restores the last valid backup when possible. Version 1.0 does not expose, upload, or share the recovery snapshot.

Deleting data

Use More → Delete all local data to remove case files, the unit preference, primary and backup archives, the recovery snapshot, and prepared temporary exports after confirmation. Deleting the app also removes its local container under normal iOS behavior. Copies exported elsewhere must be deleted separately.

External links

Opening the privacy page, support page, or email-support link leaves the app. The destination website, browser, or email provider may process ordinary request metadata under its own policy. DOA: Run Debrief does not attach a case file or reflection automatically.

Patterns

Pattern summaries are calculated on device from the records you entered and stay hidden until at least three records exist. A friction tag is shown as recurring only when it appears in at least two case files. These are simple descriptive counts, not AI analysis, medical advice, diagnosis, prediction, or coaching.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. It is a general-audience personal utility.

Security and safety limits

Local storage reduces exposure, but no device or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. The app does not monitor a run, detect emergencies or injuries, verify an entry, or provide medical, safety, or coaching advice.

Policy changes

If a future version changes its data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before those changes are released.
