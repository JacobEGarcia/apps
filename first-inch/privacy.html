Privacy policy · Version 1.0

Your first move stays yours.

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Short version: First Inch has no developer account system, backend, analytics, advertising, or tracking. The developer does not collect your intention or completion data.

Information stored on your device

First Inch may store your optional first name, intentions and generated steps, friction and energy choices, current sprint start, completion history, and reminder preference. These fields support the app’s local features.

Local storage

First Inch writes an atomic local file protected with iOS Complete Data Protection and excluded from backups. It does not use a writable preferences fallback. If protected storage is unavailable or existing data cannot be read, changes are disabled so uncertain data is not overwritten. The app shows a storage notice and remains read-only until you explicitly choose to erase app data. A failed save leaves the prior stored and visible state intact.

When migrating older preference data, First Inch removes the old copy only after the protected write succeeds. If a protected file cannot be decoded, the app reports the problem and does not silently delete it.

On-device safety check

Before creating a step, First Inch checks for a narrow set of direct personal crisis-language phrases on the device. Flagged text is not turned into a productivity step or written into the saved snapshot. Returning from the support screen clears that typed text. The check can miss relevant text or flag unrelated text and cannot assess safety.

Notifications

If you enable a daily reminder, iOS asks for notification permission and handles the local notification. Reminder changes are processed in order and the requested state is reconciled when the app returns to the foreground. If permission or scheduling fails, the reminder stays off. The developer does not receive notification data.

Exports and sharing

A JSON export is created only when you request it. It contains the complete stored snapshot, including any optional name, intentions/steps, completion history, sprint state, and reminder preference. It is not a sanitized summary. Review it before choosing a destination in the system file exporter.

Completion shares, trusted-person shares, crisis calls or texts, official-support websites, and the fixed First Inch Support link in You → Legal are user initiated. iOS and the selected destination, carrier, browser, website, or sharing provider may process ordinary request information under their own privacy terms. First Inch does not attach an export, intention, completion, or other app data automatically to those links.

Developer collection, advertising, and tracking

Version 1.0 contains no developer backend, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, data broker, cross-app tracking, account, cloud sync, or purchase flow. The developer does not sell or share app data.

Your controls and deletion

Clear all history removes completed starts while leaving other settings intact. Erase app data… first removes protected and legacy local state. Only after removal succeeds does the app cancel the local reminder and return to onboarding. If removal fails, the app keeps the reminder and visible state and reports failure instead of claiming success.

Deleting the app removes its local container under normal iOS behavior. Files you exported or information you sent through another service must be deleted from those destinations separately.

Children

The developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. First Inch is a general-audience productivity utility.

Safety and medical limits

First Inch is not medical or mental-health care, emergency monitoring, or a crisis service. It cannot diagnose, assess risk, guarantee safety, or determine whether a suggested action is appropriate. If anyone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Changes

Material changes to these practices will be reflected in this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure before release.
