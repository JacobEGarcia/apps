Reorg Rumble Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Summary

Reorg Rumble collects no personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no purchases, no cloud sync, and no network gameplay. Your progress stays on your device unless you choose to share an export.

Data stored on your device

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This data never leaves your device on its own and is deleted when you delete the app.

Data shared by you

If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and message and use them only to respond.

Third parties

Reorg Rumble integrates no third-party SDKs and shares no data with third parties.
